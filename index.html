Disregarded as proteomics

We do not keep proteomics, vendor or converted, and mixed deposits exist — a MassIVE study can carry a proteomics run beside the metabolomics one. A folder is judged by the precursor charge distribution of its converted files: proteomics DDA selects tryptic peptides, overwhelmingly 2+ and 3+, while metabolomics precursors are almost all 1+. One confident proteomics acquisition disregards its whole folder. 0 folder(s) so far.

Not by filename. Searching 219,887 staged paths for proteomics words matched 368 files, every one a bile acid–peptide conjugate — metabolomics. Peptide chemistry is legitimate subject matter here, so a name says nothing about the assay. Note also that DIA and MS1-only acquisitions record no precursor charge at all, so they cannot be judged this way and are left alone.

Archive hand-off · vendor originals

The second, independent movement: the original deposit goes to the staging server's archive area, and only then may its vendor originals be deleted here. It runs beside conversion and shares nothing with the hand-off above — a file in one area does not count as uploaded in the other. A dataset is archived whatever its conversion state; what the conversion state gates is the local delete, because a raw we have not converted yet is the only thing that can still produce an open file, and the archive offers no download.

Disk headroom

Two volumes with separate limits, so they are read separately. Botnet holds data-download — every vendor original we keep — and carries the 5,120 GiB (~5.50 TB) floor; its freed blocks are not returned promptly, so it only ever grows. gscratch holds data-ready, which mzML passes through on its way to WCH; it does release space on deletion, and it is shared with other users, so the free figure moves for reasons that have nothing to do with us.
